🌸 Bloom Home Dashboard Prompt

Build the Home Dashboard for a modern student productivity web app called Bloom.

Brand Philosophy

Bloom is a calm, minimalist study platform.

Its philosophy is:

"Studying is rewarded with better studying."

The app should feel peaceful, elegant, and cozy rather than energetic or game-like.

Think:

Apple Health
Notion
Headspace
Linear

Avoid:

Busy dashboards
Bright colors
Neon gradients
Gaming aesthetics
XP bars
Coins
Loud animations

The dashboard should make students feel calm the moment they open the app.
