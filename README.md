# 🌱 Bloom Study OS

> **A student-first academic workspace designed to help you plan, learn, track, and improve — all in one place.**

Bloom Study OS is an all-in-one study platform built around one simple idea:

**When students study, they should get better at studying.**

Instead of spreading your academic life across calendars, note apps, flashcard tools, timers, grade trackers, and analytics dashboards, Bloom brings the important parts together into one calm, focused workspace.

---

## ✨ What is Bloom?

Bloom is a **personal academic operating system** for students.

It combines:

- 📅 Planning & exams
- 📔 Notes & study guides
- 🧠 Active recall
- ❌ Mistake tracking
- 🎯 Mastery tracking
- ⏱️ Focus tracking
- 📊 Academic analytics
- 🤖 AI-powered study tools
- 🦉 A study companion
- 🌿 Interactive learning experiences

The goal isn't to add more productivity features.

The goal is to make studying **clearer, more organized, and more effective.**

---

## 🌸 The Philosophy

Bloom follows a **student-first design philosophy**.

### 🧠 Learn first

The platform is designed around actual learning rather than engagement for its own sake.

### 🌿 Stay focused

The workspace should feel calm, clean, and distraction-free.

### 📈 Understand your progress

Bloom turns your study activity into useful information about your learning.

### 📝 Learn from mistakes

Mistakes aren't just something to record.

They're information about what needs to improve.

### 🤖 AI should assist, not replace learning

AI is designed to support studying rather than become the entire studying process.

### 💚 Core tools should remain accessible

Planning, notes, tracking, and other fundamental academic utilities should not be hidden behind unnecessary paywalls.

# 🧩 Core Systems

## 📊 Dashboard

Your academic command center.

The dashboard brings together the information you need most:

- 👋 Personalized greeting
- 📅 Current date
- 🎯 Today's study goal
- 📈 Weekly progress
- 🧠 Mastery snapshot
- ⏱️ Focus insights
- 📝 Upcoming exams
- 🦉 Bloom companion
- 📊 Study analytics

### Focus insights

Dashboard focus analytics are powered by real Focus Tracker data.

They include:

- Study hours by subject
- Study hours over time
- Study quality over time
- Total study time
- Number of sessions
- Longest session

There is **no fake or duplicated analytics data**.
